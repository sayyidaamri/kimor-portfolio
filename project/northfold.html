NORTHFOLD

About

I had been watching Ines Kova and Bram Hollis build Northfold for a long time, so redesigning their studio site felt like a gift. Their identity is loose, abstract and very alive, and the job was to keep that energy while making the site calm to use. Every detail, from the grid and the type motion to the page transitions and hover states, was tuned to feel like them.
